This is a tiny Mac. It works best on a computer.

Try it

  • Hover the little window to see its controls.
  • Drag it anywhere, or resize it from a corner.
  • Unpin it, then click the editor: it slips behind. Pin it again and it floats.
  • Close it, then hover the moon in the menu bar to peek.
  • Click the moon to see what happened while you weren't looking.
  • Wait for a notification, then click it.

Make something happen

    Why I made it

    I'm a new dad. I didn't want videos of my son on someone else's servers, and the baby monitors I tried had grainy video and clumsy apps.

    So I bought a few Reolink zoom cameras and kept everything on my home network. ReoBaby replaces the Reolink app on my Mac: a small window in the corner of my screen, so I can keep an eye on my little dude while I work.

    Kilian

    What it does

    Floats like picture-in-picture

    A frameless window you can make as small as you like and pin above everything else. The controls appear only when you hover.

    Lives in the menu bar

    Hover the moon in the menu bar to peek at the camera, even with the window closed. Click the peek to keep it.

    Tells you when something happens

    Motion, people and sound, each with a snapshot. Sound is measured on your Mac, even while muted. One notification per camera every five minutes, not twenty.

    Stays on your network

    ReoBaby talks straight to your cameras. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Passwords stay in your Mac's keychain.

    Sharp and quick

    Full-resolution H.264 and H.265, decoded by your Mac's hardware, less than half a second behind the room.

    Only what you need

    Switch cameras, mute and change the volume. Arrow keys switch cameras, M mutes, T pins the window on top.

    Install

    1. Download ReoBaby.zip, unzip it and drag ReoBaby to your Applications folder.
    2. Open it. The first time, macOS refuses because ReoBaby isn't notarized by Apple. Click Done, then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
    3. Allow Local Network access so ReoBaby can reach your cameras, and allow notifications if you want alerts.
    4. Click the moon in the menu bar, choose Edit Cameras… and add each camera's address. ReoBaby asks for the password the first time it connects.

    Add your cameras

    {
  "cameras": [
    { "url": "rtsp://192.168.1.20:554/Preview_01_main",
      "user": "admin" }
  ]
}

    Turn on RTSP in each camera's settings first. In the Reolink app it's under the camera's network settings (often Advanced › Server Settings, depending on firmware). Camera names and motion alerts also need HTTP or HTTPS turned on there.

    Build it yourself

    You need macOS 14 or later and Xcode or its Command Line Tools. There are no other dependencies.

    git clone https://github.com/kilianc/reobaby.git
cd reobaby
make install

    This builds ReoBaby.app and copies it to ~/Applications. The README covers the configuration file, keyboard shortcuts and debugging tools.
