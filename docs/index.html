I'm a new dad. I didn't want videos of my son on someone else's servers, and the baby monitors I tried had grainy video and clumsy apps.

So I bought a few Reolink zoom cameras and kept everything on my home network. ReoBaby replaces the Reolink app on my Mac: a small window in the corner of my screen, so I can keep an eye on my little dude while I work.
